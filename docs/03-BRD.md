# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Purpose
Define the business requirements for SPPGPB CENTER.

## Objectives
- Integrate SPPG administration.
- Standardize workflows.
- Reduce manual work.
- Improve reporting accuracy.

## Modules
1. Dashboard
2. Master Data
3. Operations
4. Administration
5. Document Center
6. Reporting
7. Settings
8. AI Center (Future)

## Primary Actors
- Yayasan
- Kepala SPPG
- Aslap
- Ahli Gizi
- Akuntan
- Relawan

## Success Criteria
- Single source of truth
- Role-based access control
- Automatic document numbering
- Complete audit trail
