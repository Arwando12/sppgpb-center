# Business Overview

## Background
SPPG requires an integrated platform to manage administration, volunteers, beneficiary data, operations, and reporting.

## Objectives
- Standardize operational processes.
- Eliminate duplicate data.
- Centralize document management.
- Improve reporting accuracy.

## Stakeholders
- Yayasan
- Kepala SPPG
- Asisten Lapangan (Aslap)
- Ahli Gizi
- Akuntan
- Relawan
- Sekolah

## Business Scope
1. Master Data
2. Operations
3. Administration
4. Document Center
5. Reporting
6. AI Center (future)
